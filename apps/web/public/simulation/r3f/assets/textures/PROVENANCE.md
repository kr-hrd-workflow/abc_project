# Stage 4 Texture Provenance

Generated locally for the R3F dashboard asset pipeline on 2026-06-17 with the repo-installed `sharp` package and deterministic project-authored procedural routines.

No Image Gen, external downloads, purchases, stock assets, or third-party texture sources were used. The maps are authored from seeded noise, geometric masks, erosion masks, paver/window grids, and hand-defined decal sheet layout rules.

Seed: `0x4a17c9d3`

Runtime maps:
- `wet_asphalt_albedo.webp`: cool dark asphalt albedo with aggregate speckle, tar streaking, and wet pooled sheen variation.
- `wet_asphalt_roughness.webp`: grayscale roughness-style map aligned to wet pooling, with lower roughness in wet streaks and higher aggregate grit.
- `worn_lane_markings.png`: transparent decal sheet with worn white/yellow lane segments, stop-bar fragments, and arrow geometry.
- `crosswalk_wear.png`: transparent crosswalk decal sheet with eroded zebra bars and tire-wear bands.
- `curb_grime.png`: transparent curb grime strip with lower-edge dirt buildup and vertical runoff/drip variation.
- `sidewalk_paver_variation.webp`: paver material variation with staggered seams, mottling, stains, and small cracks.
- `facade_window_emissive.webp`: facade/window emissive sheet with dark mullions and varied warm/cool lit panes.
