  reconstructions of the real 강남역 사거리 (Gangnam Station intersection):
  landmark forms and infrastructure (강남대로 bidirectional central red median

  traffic signals, pedestrian signals, 4-way zebra crosswalks, glowing media
  poles, LED billboards, Samsung-Town-style glass towers SW, glass-and-steel
  canyon, plane trees) are derived from the real location. All signage is
  invented Gangnam-style (clinics/academies/cafes); no real brand logos or
  trademarks are present. Background-visual-only; never a vehicle, pedestrian,
  or signal truth surface. All four plates are 1536x1024 (allowNonPowerOfTwo:
  projected onto a backdrop quad).
